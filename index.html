01 · About us

A studio for enduring work.

And Again is an architecture and engineering practice based in Copenhagen. We design buildings and calculate the structures that hold them up — under one roof, with one set of hands.

Our work begins with the place, the climate and the client, and ends in a building that quietly belongs. We work across single-family houses, residential blocks, refurbishments and small public commissions throughout Denmark.

03 · Authority projects

Everything for
approval & build.

From the first line to a finished build — sketches, visualisations, authority drawings and building permits, all under one roof.

01

Architectural drawings

Drawings with an eye for both design and detail. Plans, elevations, sections, roof plans and 3D visualisations — aesthetic solutions that are technically buildable.

  • Floor & site plans
  • Facades & sections
  • 3D visualisation
02

Structural calculations

The core of a structure's strength and stability. We analyse the forces acting on a building — compression, tension, bending and moment — across complexity classes KK1 to KK3.

  • Load-bearing walls
  • Lintels & rafters
  • Signed by a certified engineer
03

Building permit applications

We handle the building permit from start to finish, navigating the municipal process so your project meets every regulation and approval — with no unnecessary middlemen.

  • Authority drawings
  • Local plan compliance
  • Municipal liaison
04

Digitisation of archive drawings

We convert old physical plans into secure, future-proof digital files — site plans, floor plans, facades, sections and change drawings — so they're never lost again.

05

Profitability calculation

A clear economic analysis of where an energy renovation pays off best — renovation costs, potential savings and the payback period, in black and white.

06

Heat loss calculation

A detailed analysis of heat lost through the building envelope — walls, roof, windows and floors — identifying thermal bridges and guiding smart renovation investment.
