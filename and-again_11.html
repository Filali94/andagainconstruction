01 · About us

A studio for
enduring work.

And Again is an architecture and engineering practice based in Copenhagen. We design buildings and calculate the structures that hold them up — under one roof, with one set of hands.

Our work begins with the place, the climate and the client, and ends in a building that quietly belongs. We work across single-family houses, residential blocks, refurbishments and small public commissions throughout Denmark.

05 · How we work

From sketch
to keys.

01

First conversation

A 30-minute meeting — in person or on Google Meet — to understand your site, your brief and your dreams for the project. Always free.

30 min · Free
02

Sketch proposal

A first design proposal with plans, sections and a rough estimate of cost. We sit down, walk through it, and you decide if it feels right.

2–4 weeks
03

Permit drawings

We prepare and submit the building permit application (byggetilladelse) to the local municipality with all required documentation.

4–8 weeks
04

Structural calculation

Eurocode-compliant calculation report for the structure, signed by our certified structural engineer (certificeret statiker).

3–6 weeks
05

Construction documents

Detailed drawings, material specifications and tender documents — everything the contractor needs to build the project well.

6–10 weeks
06

On-site supervision

Regular site visits during construction to ensure the project is built as drawn — and to solve the small surprises that always appear.
